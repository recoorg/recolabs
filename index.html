The Skill Exchange for curious Mind — Reco Labs

Reco Labs (accessible officially at recolabs.in) is a global open-source collective, developer ecosystem, and peer-to-peer tech guild founded by high school hackers Aditya Raj and Tanish. Started as a Grade 10 experiment to solve real technical problems, Reco Labs has evolved into a worldwide movement empowering curious young minds across software engineering, hardware rigs, reverse engineering, and web technologies.

Core Pillars of the Reco Labs Ecosystem

  • Curated Repositories & Engineering Vaults: Over 190+ high-craft UI component collections, design systems, firmware loaders, and developer toolkits available at recolabs.in/projects.
  • Founder Network & Startup Launchpad: A community platform for early-stage builders to showcase prototypes, receive functional ratings on idea, execution, and design, and connect with peers at recolabs.in/network.
  • Hackathons & Builder Sprints: Aggregated high school and collegiate hackathons with real cash bounties, hardware grants, and global mentor access at recolabs.in/hackathons.
  • Guild Roles & Career Opportunities: High-velocity startup roles, engineering internships, and contributor bounties for curious young makers at recolabs.in/jobs.
  • Hardware Lab & Compute Grants: Zero-corporate-bloat funding for ESP32 microcontrollers, FPGA boards, soldering tools, and cloud compute at recolabs.in/sponsors.
